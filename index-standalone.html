About Me

The story so far

I'm a results-driven Technical Project Manager and Certified Scrum Master with over seven years of experience leading cross-functional teams across aerospace, tech, and the nonprofit sector. At Airbus, I've built supplier scoring models, designed KPI dashboards, and driven procurement process improvements that measurably improved efficiency, forecasting accuracy, and supply chain resilience.

My work has always lived at the intersection of operations and analytics — and now I'm formalizing that edge through a Master of Science in Data Science (Policy, Governance & Society) at Brown University, expected May 2027. I'm most excited about applying data science to policy problems where the stakes are real: procurement equity, AI governance, and the economics of reshoring.
